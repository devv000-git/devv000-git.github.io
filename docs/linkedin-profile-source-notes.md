## Important boundaries

- The CloudVantage entry on the screenshot includes a bullet about an Azure-hosted React and Python Azure Functions portfolio solution, but that bullet ends in a **“more”** truncation. We have **not invented the missing remainder**.
- The three capstone project titles and dates are visible in LinkedIn, but detailed source files, screenshots and measurements have **not been provided**. The website displays them as LinkedIn-listed work with technical evidence still pending.
- Hardhat Enterprises work is identified as an **industry capstone**, not as a conventional full-time staff position.
- The Deloitte Australia Cyber Job Simulation (Forage) is categorized as a **job simulation**, not employment.
- Certifications and learning programmes are grouped as shown in the PDF, with the vendor certification distinguished from professional certificates, courses and simulations.
- The PDF shows the university education ending in November 2026. The portfolio owner's earlier direct statement was that degree requirements were completed in October 2026, with formal graduation expected in February 2027. The public website displays **2026**, avoiding a contested month.
- The source LinkedIn PDF is a **rendering of a profile page**, not a full data archive. Portions of experience descriptions and project details may not be visible.
- A feed item about another person's Master's graduation appears in the Activity section. It is **not** the portfolio owner's education and must not be attributed to them.
- Other LinkedIn members shown in "similar profiles" are unrelated and excluded.
